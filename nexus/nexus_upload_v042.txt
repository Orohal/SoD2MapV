[size=3][b]SoD2MapViewer v0.4.2 — framework-dependent (unpacked variant)[/b][/size]

[b]This is the unpacked/preferred variant for users whose antivirus flags
single-file executables as a false positive.[/b]

Extract anywhere and run [b]SoD2MapViewer.exe[/b]. Requires the
[b].NET 10 Desktop Runtime (x64)[/b]:
https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0

What's new in v0.4.2:
[list][*]Map background for all 5 regions — faustite (Trumbull Valley),
amethyst (Cascade Hills), basalt (Drucker County), copper (Meagher
Valley), danburite (Providence Ridge). The region is picked
automatically from the loaded map path.
[*]Tile cache is now pinned per region — an earlier cache mix-up could
show foreign tiles/holes after switching regions.
[*]Tile drawing: coarse level first, zoom limited to the reliable CDN
levels (z4/z5), missing tiles upscaled from the parent level.
[*]WORK IN PROGRESS: the background tile CDN is currently incomplete
server-side (tiles vanish between requests) — holes in the background
are possible. A community basemap fallback is planned.[/list]
